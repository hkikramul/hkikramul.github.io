01 / Our purpose

Built for discovery.
Governed for the public good.

To advance human health through independent, rigorous, and collaborative research.

The vision is a lasting scientific community: a place where theoretical depth meets real biomedical questions, and where researchers have the freedom and support to pursue meaningful work.

An independent board of trustees would steward the mission, while scientific leadership and researchers shape the work through evidence, ethics, and scholarly judgment.

03 / Proposed governance

Independent by design.

A trustee-governed institution with clear responsibilities, transparent decisions, and scientific freedom. This is the intended structure; no board or advisory appointments are announced.

01

Board of trustees

Stewards the mission, oversees finances and institutional risk, and appoints and evaluates the director.

02

Institute director

Leads operations, recruitment, partnerships, and implementation within board-approved strategy and budgets.

03

Scientific advisory council

Provides independent assessment of scientific quality and guidance on research priorities.

04

Research community

Conducts research with scholarly independence, responsible data practices, and appropriate ethics oversight.

“The trustees protect the conditions for good science. The evidence determines its conclusions.”

Founding commitments: conflict-of-interest safeguards, fair appointments, financial accountability, inclusive participation, and freedom from donor or partisan control.

04 / A long-term undertaking

Start with people.
Grow through good science.

FIRST / Foundations

Clarify the mission

Develop the founding concept, deepen scientific expertise, and build relationships around shared research interests.

NEXT / Collaboration

Build a research network

Begin focused collaborative projects with appropriate institutional partnerships and responsible oversight.

LATER / Institution

Establish a lasting home

Formalize governance and legal status, secure sustainable funding, and expand when scientific needs and resources justify it.

Development will follow readiness and resources rather than a fixed launch date. No recruitment, clinical services, or fundraising campaign is currently offered through this website.
