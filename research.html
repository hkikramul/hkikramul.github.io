PUBLIC-HEALTH RESEARCH · PREPRINT

Cohabitation timing &
adolescent childbearing

“Cohabitation timing and childbearing or current pregnancy among married adolescents in Bangladesh: a complex-survey analysis of the 2022 BDHS.”

Complex-survey analysis of the association between cohabitation timing and childbearing or current pregnancy among married adolescents in Bangladesh, using the 2022 BDHS. The study accounts for the survey sampling design and examines associations rather than causal effects.

Read manuscripts and version history ↗Authors: Md Ikramul Haq Khan, Israt Jahan, Mohammad Amirul Islam, Rohan Dalpatadu, Mohammad Karim.

to epidemic modeling

Developing a thesis direction around epidemic models, stochastic components, and the estimation of infection-rate behavior.

RESEARCH PROPOSAL · IN DEVELOPMENT

Evaluating AI-generated regression analyses

Developing a study of whether AI systems produce statistically defensible biomedical regression analyses. Current work focuses on reviewing existing evaluation tasks and designing a bounded simulation pilot. Novelty and feasibility remain under review.

Proposal in development; novelty and feasibility under review.
EXPLORATORY ANALYSIS · DRAFT UNDER REVIEW

Contraceptive continuity after method discontinuation

A BDHS 2022 calendar-based study of switching, subsequent nonuse and recorded pregnancy after selected contraceptive discontinuations. The analysis combines complex-survey estimation, sensitivity to population definitions, and grouped model validation.

Draft under review. Separate from the adolescent-childbearing preprint.
