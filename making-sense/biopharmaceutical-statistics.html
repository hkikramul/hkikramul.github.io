FOUNDATIONS AND PROFESSIONAL DISCOVERY

Resources to return to.

  • FDA / ICH E9: Statistical Principles for Clinical Trials — a foundation for understanding statistical planning and analysis in trials.
  • ICH E9(R1): Estimands and Sensitivity Analysis — connect the clinical question, treatment effect of interest, analysis, and sensitivity assessment.
  • FDA: Bayesian Statistics in Medical Device Clinical Trials — a device-specific guidance resource; do not assume it governs all drug trials.
  • Hernán and Robins: Causal Inference: What If — freely available author-hosted textbook and computing materials.
  • ASA accredited-member roster — discover PStat and GStat holders, then investigate their work through professional sources. Accreditation is one discovery route, not a requirement for inclusion here.

A SMALL STARTING PATH

Connect learning to a clinical question.

  1. Begin with trial fundamentals: randomization, endpoints, sample size, power, and uncertainty.
  2. Read one clinical methods presentation and identify a question that connects to regression or simulation.
  3. Use E9(R1) to distinguish the treatment effect being asked about from the analysis used to estimate it.
  4. Explore survival analysis, longitudinal outcomes, missing data, multiplicity, and statistical programming as relevant questions arise.

For each resource, record only the question, a useful method or idea, and one limitation. No additional project or recurring commitment is implied.
