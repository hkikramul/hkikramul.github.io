STATISTICALLY SPEAKING — ANALYSIS PLANNING CHECKLIST
Educational guide, not a study-specific statistical analysis plan.

1. Question: Define the population, outcome, comparison, and scientific objective.
2. Estimand: State exactly what quantity you want to estimate.
3. Design: Document sampling, randomization, clustering, and repeated observations.
4. Data: Define variables, units, exclusions, missingness, and quality checks.
5. Description: Plot distributions and summarize groups before modeling.
6. Method: Justify the model using the question, outcome, and design.
7. Assumptions: Specify diagnostics and how you will address violations.
8. Uncertainty: Plan confidence intervals and relevant error control.
9. Sensitivity: Identify reasonable alternative assumptions and analyses.
10. Reporting: Report estimates, uncertainty, limitations, and reproducible code.

For surveys, account for weights, strata, and primary sampling units.
For causal questions, document identification assumptions and potential confounding.
Distinguish prespecified analysis from exploration. Protect participant privacy.
