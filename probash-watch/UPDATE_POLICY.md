# Probash Watch automatic media updates

Public app: https://hkikramul.github.io/probash-watch/
Canonical repository: hkikramul/hkikramul.github.io, main branch, directory probash-watch/.

## User-authorized publication policy
News reports from domestic or foreign media may be published directly as source-reported coverage WITHOUT human editorial approval. Scope: Bangladeshi citizens abroad in every destination, including workers, students, seafarers and other diaspora members. Do not infer citizenship from ethnicity or birthplace. News, published NGO investigations and official statements may be included from any relevant source; sources.json is a starting registry, not an exclusive allowlist.

Individually submitted private allegations always remain moderated and separate. Never retrieve or copy private intake, complainant identity, NID/passport, medical documents, contact details or private testimony into GitHub or public data. No automatic forwarding to a legal intake team, NGOs, authorities or third parties. External organizations listed are possible referral routes, not confirmed project partners.

## Each scheduled update
1. Read current public data/news.json, data/statistics.json, data/incidents.json and sources.json through GitHub. Search recent domestic, foreign and destination-local media for relevant new coverage, follow-ups, authority responses and published statistics. Use publication dates, distinguish incident dates, and retain source-language metadata.
2. Open original source pages where possible. Unavailable full text may be indexed with a title and original link, explicitly labelled metadata-only; do not fabricate a summary. Social posts are leads, not newspaper reports. Do not bypass paywalls or copy full articles. Summaries must be short, attributed and preserve allegation language. Redact sexual-violence survivor/minor identities, exact private locations, identity numbers, contact details and unnecessary identifying combinations even if a source publishes them.
3. Append/update news records using the existing schema: id, country (ISO-2 or MULTI/UNKNOWN), published_date (YYYY-MM-DD or null), event_date, publisher, url, language, title:{bn,en}, summary:{bn,en}, record_type (incident/follow_up/investigation/policy/statistics), incident_key (nullable), status:source_reported, nationality_basis, added_at, translation_status:summary_draft, independently_verified:false. Use stable IDs and canonical URLs, retain previous records, merge exact duplicate URLs. Multiple independent publications or syndications about one event may remain separate news items, but share an incident_key only when the connection is explicit. Do not count copied articles as independent confirmation or distinct victims.
4. Add a case to incidents.json only for a specific event with clear country and an adequate date/case identity; preserve existing cases and link new sources to existing cases instead of duplicating them. Do not turn aggregate statistics or general policy stories into incident/victim totals. Unknown victim counts remain null. Separate allegation, source reporting and court findings. Never invent ministry responses or claim absence of help merely because no response was found. Published media reports do not require a human approval step.
5. Add statistics only when indicator, unit, population, country, gender, reference period, calendar/fiscal basis and source are clear. Preserve all-cause deaths separately from abuse; complaints separately from confirmed offences; returnees separately from abuse victims. Do not sum overlapping cumulative, annual and fiscal series. Do not fabricate male/female prevalence, causal attribution, missing years or current outcomes. Store unknown boundaries in the note and relative_window/range basis.
6. Commit only changed JSON data through GitHub update_file, fetching the fresh SHA for each file, preserving unrelated content. Update news.json updated_at and last_successful_scan_at only after a real successful search, including a zero-new-results search. Retain coverage_complete:false. Never change source code, branding, site access or individual-submission status during a news run.
7. Read committed data back and confirm it persisted; check the GitHub Pages deployment and report failures accurately. Do not say a queue-only update is a public update. Do not claim the archive is exhaustive or the service real-time.

## Privacy and scope
The public directory links to official missions and independent service providers. Listing verification is not testing every phone, consular jurisdiction or service availability. No project legal inbox or authorized caseworker has been configured. The complaint-draft tool is browser-memory-only and does not submit or save complaints. External complaint mechanisms have their own eligibility, disclosure and consent rules; no blanket confidentiality, enforcement or rescue is promised.
