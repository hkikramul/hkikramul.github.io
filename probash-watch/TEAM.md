# Maintenance and collaboration

Maintained by **NPA Data Analytics Team**.

In collaboration with **NPA Probashi** and **NPA American Wing**.

Public dashboard: https://hkikramul.github.io/probash-watch/

The public dashboard is readable without a login. Private reports, identity information and editorial access are not made public. This attribution does not provision team accounts or confer access to private reports. Case coverage remains focused on Bangladeshi citizen migrant workers regardless of birthplace. Evidence and allegations must remain distinct from organizational positions.
