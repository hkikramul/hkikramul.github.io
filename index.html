01 / ABOUT

A statistical foundation.
A human perspective.

I am pursuing an M.S. in Mathematical Sciences with an Applied Statistics concentration at UNLV. Alongside my studies, I serve as a graduate teaching assistant in the Department of Mathematical Sciences.

My current thesis direction is epidemic modeling, with an emphasis on statistical reasoning, simulation, and uncertainty. My research experience includes complex survey analysis, regression modeling, statistical computing, and collaborative health research using the Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey (BDHS) and the Health Information National Trends Survey (HINTS).

I am interested in developing stronger methodological foundations and connecting them with biomedical applications. My interests include causal inference, survival analysis, Bayesian methods, high-dimensional statistics, and stochastic epidemic models.

My long-term goal is a research and academic career in statistics or biostatistics, connecting methodological development with biomedical and public-health collaboration. I value reproducibility, clear scientific communication, and careful reasoning.

Beyond statistics, I enjoy evolutionary biology, psychology, photography, and the history and philosophy of science.

TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

Clarity, curiosity, and active engagement.

I help students move beyond memorizing formulas toward understanding the reasoning behind mathematical and statistical ideas. I encourage questions, connect concepts to applications, and help students build confidence with unfamiliar problems.

In statistics, I emphasize critical thinking about data, assumptions, uncertainty, and evidence. In mathematics, I connect conceptual understanding with problem-solving skills so students understand both how a method works and why it works.

I aim to create a supportive, inclusive environment where students can ask questions, learn from mistakes, and develop their own approaches. My goal is to cultivate independent thinking, quantitative reasoning, and intellectual curiosity.
