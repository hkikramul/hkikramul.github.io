01 / ABOUT

Statistical inquiry.
Thoughtful teaching.

I am an M.S. student in Mathematical Sciences, with an Applied Statistics concentration, at UNLV. My research experience includes complex survey analysis, regression modeling, statistical computing, and collaborative health research using BDHS and HINTS data.

My developing thesis direction explores epidemic modeling, simulation, and uncertainty, with Prof. Rohan Dalpatadu and Dr. Miguel Fudolig. My broader interests include causal inference, machine learning, and high-dimensional statistics for biomedical applications.

Through undergraduate discussion instruction and tutoring, I help students connect statistical and mathematical ideas with visual explanations. My long-term goal is an academic career combining methodological research, biomedical collaboration, and thoughtful mentorship.

I value reproducible analysis and clear communication of uncertainty. Outside statistics, I enjoy photography and the history and philosophy of science. My education and experience are detailed in my CV.
