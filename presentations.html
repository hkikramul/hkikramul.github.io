NOV 092026
POSTER PRESENTED

UNLV Interdisciplinary Research Symposium

“Cohabitation timing and childbearing or current pregnancy among married adolescents in Bangladesh.” This study uses the 2022 Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey to examine associations between timing of first cohabitation and having had a live birth or being currently pregnant among ever-married adolescents.

In the descriptive sample of 2,449 adolescents, 59.7% had experienced at least one live birth or were currently pregnant. These results describe the study population and should not be interpreted as causal effects.

My primary contribution: model diagnostics and validation, methodological checks that are often underreported in DHS- and MICS-based studies (Dey et al., 2025).

Authors: Md. Ikramul Haq Khan, Israt Jahan, Mohammad Amirul Islam, Rohan Dalpatadu, and Mohammad Karim.

November 9, 2026 · Poster session 2:30–4:00 p.m. · UNLV Student Union
