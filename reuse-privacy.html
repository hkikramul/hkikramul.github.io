Privacy by default

  • No advertising trackers or site analytics are loaded by this implementation. Hosting providers may still process IP addresses, request logs, and security information under their own policies.
  • Display and reduced-motion preferences are stored in this browser’s local storage, shared across these sites on this origin. Clear browser site data to remove them. No account is needed.
  • Solar timing requests geolocation only when you press its button. Coordinates stay in page memory; this site does not save or transmit them to its server. Your browser and operating system handle the permission request.
  • Page narration uses browser speech synthesis. Local voices are offered by default. Online voices require your opt-in and may send spoken page text to the voice provider. Private input fields are excluded from narration.
  • Other-language machine translation is an optional external Google service. Opening it sends the public page address and your request to Google. It is not a verified edition. Do not enter private reports or documents in that view.
  • External embedded media loads only after a click. The provider may receive your IP address and apply its own tracking or privacy terms. External links and contact forms operate under their providers’ policies.
  • Probash Watch’s public news archive is distinct from private allegations. This static site does not provide a secure intake channel. Do not submit identity numbers, passports, private testimony, medical documents, personal addresses, or other sensitive records. The complaint-draft tool stays in browser memory and is not sent by this site.

Contact ikramul.statistics@gmail.com about corrections, attribution, privacy concerns or removal requests. Give the page address and a minimal description; do not attach sensitive documents. Requests are assessed under applicable rights and obligations; removal is not guaranteed. Information already copied by third parties may remain outside our control.
