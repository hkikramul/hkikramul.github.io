Dubai Moves to Replace “Housewife” with “Generation Shaper”

East Bengal Renaissance Desk · 25 November 2026

Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Crown Prince of Dubai, has directed the emirate’s Community Development Authority to adopt the title “Generation Shaper” instead of “housewife” for women engaged in raising children and caring for their families. The announcement was made for Mother’s Day.

In a message published on 21 March 2026, Sheikh Hamdan described mothers as their children’s first school, where they learn belonging, responsibility and values. He presented the new designation as recognition of that social role.

“Generation Shaper” reframes household and caregiving work in terms of its contribution to shaping future generations. A change in title, by itself, does not establish that caregivers will receive income, social protection or public services.

The announcement names Dubai’s Community Development Authority. It does not specify which forms or services will use the designation, or whether the change applies across every government department. The available announcement therefore does not establish that the term has been replaced on all official records.
