Bangladesh’s 11 Sector Reform Commission Reports and PDFs

East Bengal Renaissance · 13 November 2026

The Bangladesh government’s reform portal lists reports from 11 core sectoral commissions. This directory links to each official report page and, where a direct government PDF address could be verified, to the PDF itself. The Constitution Reform Commission report has several volumes; the Local Government Reform Commission report is also available as a summary and two volumes. Check the title and volume before downloading.

Bangladesh Government Reform Commission portal: all reports · Cabinet Division list of commission reports

  11. Health Sector Reform Commission — official report page · full report PDF (322 pages).

How to use this directory

This page is an index to the government reports, not a summary of their recommendations or an implementation review. A commission recommendation should not be treated as enacted law or a government decision. Check legislation, gazettes, official action plans and current implementation records to determine what has been adopted or carried out.

Sources checked on 13 November 2026. Links point to the government reform portal, the relevant ministry or commission, and the Commission chair’s report page where noted.
