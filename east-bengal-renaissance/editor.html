About the author and editor

Md. Ikramul Haq Khan, GStat
Author and editor, East Bengal Renaissance

Khan is an M.S. student in Mathematical Sciences, concentrating in Applied Statistics, and a graduate teaching assistant at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas. His research interests connect statistical methodology, epidemic modeling, and biomedical and public-health questions. He holds the American Statistical Association’s Graduate Statistician (GStat) designation.

He created Making Sense with Ikramul to make statistics, research, science, and social questions accessible through clear explanations. His public writing explores evidence, education, labor, equality, and human dignity.
