AI Policy for Bangladesh: From Social Democratic Perspective

A public-interest proposal for policy and political discussion. Sources reviewed through 24 November 2026.

The proposal at a glance

Bangladesh should use AI to improve public services, expand access to knowledge in Bangla and raise productivity. A social democratic approach asks who gets the gains, who can challenge mistakes and how workers participate in decisions about their jobs.

Proposed position for adoption
Support public investment in AI for Bangla and other local languages, independent testing and registration of high-risk systems, meaningful appeals, advance consultation with workers and fair sharing of productivity gains. Name a public authority responsible for every publicly funded high-risk deployment.
  • Start now. Require audit rights, a public register and a fallback in public procurement.
  • Test before scaling. Pilot an official information chatbot and a tool that helps staff check flood-relief documents.
  • Legislate safeguards. Establish clear rules on notice, independent review, remedies and prohibited uses.

The proposed duties, risk tiers and implementation dates are for discussion.

1 · Bangladesh’s policy and legal starting point

The official AI policy portal reports that a 16-member drafting committee was formed on 2 November 2026. Its deadline to submit the draft is 15 December 2026. A public comment period or submission channel should be confirmed separately through official announcements. 3 · A high-risk testing protocol modelled on biomedical research

Use the Declaration of Helsinki as a model for independent ethics review and protection of participants, SPIRIT-AI for writing the plan before testing, and CONSORT-AI for reporting results. Compare the proposed system with the current service. Clinical research must follow applicable research ethics and clinical approvals. [10–12]

Evaluation design

Specify the main public outcome in advance. A relief pilot might aim to shorten waiting times while keeping wrongful exclusion below an agreed limit. Compare both the current workflow and a simpler digital alternative on the same terms. Keep records for the same person or household out of both training and test sets. Validate against different locations and time periods. Where ethical and feasible, randomise at the service-site level or use a staged rollout. For other designs, document differences between comparison groups and uncertainty in the results.

Measures, sample and approval

Wrongful exclusion is the number of eligible people wrongly rejected divided by the number of eligible people. Establish eligibility independently of the model. Appeal records alone miss people who never file. Plan enough observations for important groups and publish counts, denominators, confidence intervals and gaps in the evidence. Set acceptance thresholds for overall and group-specific errors before the pilot and record serious harms separately.

Generative AI

Show the source and date when answers come from official documents. Hand off to a person when the answer or source is uncertain. Test fabricated answers, faulty citations, language coverage, low bandwidth, accessibility, data leakage and prevention of unauthorised actions. Limit access to relevant data. Require separate human authorisation for payments, record deletion and eligibility denial. Repeat critical checks before adopting a major model change. [9]

Incidents and recovery

Give a named official authority to suspend affected use after serious harm, a data breach, unacceptable subgroup errors, a service outage or failed appeals. Activate the fallback, preserve evidence, notify the relevant authorities and affected people as applicable, review past decisions and make remedies available. Independent checks and written approval precede a restart.

Draft clauses for discussion

  1. Registration and testing. A public body may deploy a high-risk system after independent review, relevant local validation and publication of a summary that protects personal data and lawful confidentiality.
  2. Human decision and appeal. A designated authority must explain a denial of essential services and offer review by a person with the power to correct the decision.
  3. Worker participation. Before significant changes to employment conditions, work allocation or monitoring, employers must provide relevant information, consult representatives and document the steps taken.
  4. Approval and expiry. High-risk approvals are time-limited. Major changes to use, version or risk require reapproval. Authorities publish decision and incident summaries on a defined schedule.

6 · Delivery, budget and first-year timeline

First 90 daysICT Division and procurement authorities prepare a register, risk guidance and model tender terms. Consult the public.
Months 4–9Run two pilots with independent reviewers. Keep phone and in-person support available.
Months 10–12Publish outcomes, costs and incidents. Decide what to change, suspend or scale and identify gaps in legal authority.

Institutional responsibility

The ICT Division coordinates common standards and the register. Service agencies own outcomes and remedies. Sector regulators act within their powers. Independent high-risk review should include technical, legal, rights and sector experts and representatives of affected people. Require conflict disclosure, recusal and a record of dissent. Establish the legal basis for mandatory approval, suspension and penalties.

Costs and environmental impact

Budget for licences, data preparation, computing, local validation, staff training, audits, human service channels, appeals, maintenance and exit. Compare total cost per correctly completed service. Report who benefits, hours saved or added and uncertainty in the estimates. Require large vendors to explain electricity and water use and their measurement boundaries. Estimate pilot costs from the design and actual bids.

Public procurement

Contracts should guarantee independent testing, data return and deletion, disclosure of subcontractors and processing locations, incident notice, retesting before major version changes, documentation and transferability. Practise supplier exit while maintaining service. A public register should show purpose, accountable agency, version, evaluation summary and complaint channel. [6] [8]

7 · Decisions for the political discussion

Suggested 60 minutes. 10 for the position, 15 for rights, 15 for labour and investment, 15 for implementation and 5 to confirm decisions.

  1. Will notice and appeals be mandatory? How quickly must people receive review for urgent care or relief?
  2. How should prohibitions on mass surveillance, social scoring and fully automated denials be defined? Who oversees them?
  3. How will contract and platform workers participate? How will gains be shared through pay, working hours and training?
  4. Should existing institutions be strengthened or a new body established in law? How will appointments, funding and conflicts of interest be handled?
  5. Which agency will run the first pilot? Who approves the budget, success thresholds and authority to stop?
Record the outcome
Write down agreed principles, amendments, disagreements, owners for the policy submission and the next review date.
