Executed biomedical example

The public Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer dataset contains 569 observations and 30 numerical measurements. The reproducible split has 455 training and 114 test observations. The first two components retain 63.4% of training variance; ten retain 95%. Labels are added after PCA for interpretation.

Training cross-validation selected five PCs with regularized logistic regression. Test AUC was 0.9934 and Brier score 0.0247. These are educational results from one split, not a validated clinical tool. The notes explain limitations and appropriate external validation.

Use the method for a defined decision

High variance is not automatically predictive value or biological importance. Decide whether the objective is exploration, compression, prediction or monitoring; validate that objective. Fit imputation, scaling and PCA using training observations only. Investigate batch effects, outliers and unstable eigendirections before interpreting components.
