PROGRAM PROFILE 01

Virginia Tech

Statistics, Ph.D. · Fall 2027 planning

Virginia Tech’s Statistics Ph.D. includes concentrations in general statistical methodology and theory, biostatistics, computational statistics, and other application areas. This profile summarizes the current admissions information relevant to preparing an application.

APPLICATION TIMING

Deadline

The Department of Statistics lists January 15 for August admission and recommends submitting all materials by then for full consideration, including financial assistance. For Fall 2027 planning, use January 15, 2027 as the target and confirm it in the live application portal.

RECOMMENDATIONS

Three letters

The department requires three recommendation letters and strongly encourages online submissions. The application sends invitations to recommenders when their information is entered.

TRANSCRIPTS

Upload readable scans

Upload a scanned transcript from each institution where you earned or will earn an undergraduate or graduate degree. Unofficial copies are accepted for admission review. Each scan must clearly show the institution name, your name, course names, and grades; scan both sides so grading-scale and credit information are visible.

AFTER AN OFFER

Official final records

After an offer, arrange for official transcripts showing degree conferral to be sent directly by the institution’s Registrar’s Office, by sealed mail or an eTranscript service. The Graduate School’s current transcript page says final official transcripts are due before enrollment.

STANDARDIZED TESTS

GRE and English proficiency

The Statistics department describes the GRE as recommended, not required. International applicants must meet the Graduate School’s English-proficiency requirement unless eligible for a waiver. The current Graduate School page lists TOEFL iBT 4.5 or IELTS 6.5; the Statistics admissions page still lists the older TOEFL iBT 90 benchmark. Check the current Graduate School page and confirm the applicable score or waiver for your record.

PROGRAM FIT

Research concentrations

The Ph.D. offers training in statistical methodology and theory, biostatistics, computational statistics, and other areas. The methodology and theory concentration allows substantial flexibility in coursework within and outside the department.
