Accessibility

Read, listen, and choose your settings.

Open “Reading tools” near the start of each page for display, language, speech and music controls. These features supplement your browser and screen reader; they are not a claim of complete WCAG conformance.

  • Use the skip link to reach the main content. Buttons, links, selectors and audio controls work with a keyboard and show a visible focus outline.
  • Automatic light adjustment uses the same algorithm across every site. Choose Light or Dark to override it. Automatic uses your local clock, or solar elevation if you explicitly permit location.
  • Speech reads visible article text, excluding private form inputs. Choose a voice matching the current page language. If no voice is available, install one through your device or explicitly allow an online voice. We cannot guarantee Bangla or other language voices on every device.
  • Listen, pause/resume, and stop are separate controls. Escape stops speech and ambient music. Music also stops when narration starts or the page becomes hidden.
  • Ambient music is optional and off by default. It has separate volume and stop controls. The national anthem has its own native audio player and is not part of ambient music.
  • Motion is brief and illustrative. Operating-system reduced-motion preferences are respected, and the shared Reduce Motion setting disables animations and smooth scrolling.
  • Bangla and English editions share the same page structure, images and reading order. Some archive articles already contain both languages; linked published translations remain available. Newly prepared archive translations should be checked against sources for consequential use.
  • Probash Watch has native Bangla/English interfaces. Country-language choices offer clearly labelled external machine translations, which may be incomplete or affect interactive features.

Report a barrier with the page URL and your device/browser to ikramul.statistics@gmail.com. Avoid sharing private reports or medical information.
