PUBLISHING RESOURCES

APC-free publishing and open-access support.

Resources for planning future statistics and biomedical publications. Open access means free to read; author fees and funding coverage must be checked separately.

  • Directory of Open Access Journals — search for journals without article processing charges, then confirm all charges on the journal’s own website.
  • UNLV Open Article Fund and APC support — check current funding eligibility and publisher agreement coverage before committing to fees.
  • School of Medicine Library Open Article Fund — review current affiliation and applicant requirements; a biomedical research topic alone does not establish eligibility.
  • Think. Check. Submit. — evaluate journal credibility, peer review, transparent fees, and suitability before submission.
  • APC 101: How do I publish an open access article? — public UNLV workshop presentation.

Publication planning checklist

  1. Choose journals for scientific fit and credible peer review.
  2. Check for a no-APC option, institutional agreement, waiver, or eligible funding; confirm total costs and obtain any required approval before incurring fees.
  3. Confirm corresponding-author and affiliation requirements for institutional coverage.
  4. Check whether the accepted manuscript can be shared in a repository, including permitted version and embargo.
